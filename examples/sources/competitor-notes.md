# Competitor scan — rough notes (internal, don't share)

Quick look at what else is out there for the freelancer-money space. Not exhaustive.

**YNAB** — the 800lb gorilla of "intentional" budgeting. Powerful, devoted fanbase. BUT: steep learning curve, lots of manual envelope assignment. Our churned users specifically cite "too much work." Built for disciplined salaried people. Doesn't do tax set-aside or invoice-aware runway. Opportunity: they're the opposite of low-effort.

**Mint** — RIP, shut down. Big pool of orphaned users looking for a home. They were free + ad-supported + passive. These people want low effort. Worth chasing but they're cheapskates by definition.

**Monarch / Copilot** — pretty dashboards, premium feel, couples/households focus. Strong on charts. Weak on the irregular-income / self-employed use case. Not really aimed at freelancers.

**QuickBooks Self-Employed** — actually does tax estimates + mileage + invoices. This is the real threat on the "tax" angle. BUT it's accounting software, feels like work, expensive, aimed at filing not at daily peace-of-mind. People use it because they have to, not because they like it.

**"I just use a spreadsheet"** — genuinely our biggest competitor. Free, infinitely flexible, and multiple interviewees admitted to maintaining one ALONGSIDE paying us. That's damning. If people pay us AND keep a spreadsheet, we're not answering their actual question.

## Takeaways
- Nobody owns "low-effort peace of mind for irregular income." YNAB is high-effort, QBSE is work, Monarch is for couples.
- The tax-set-aside feature is defensible vs everyone except QuickBooks, and QB makes it feel like a chore.
- "Runway for freelancers" is basically an open lane.
- Our moat candidate = answer the one question (am I ok?) with zero manual work, via bank sync we already have.
