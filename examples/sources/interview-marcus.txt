User interview — Marcus (freelance developer, churned last month)
[notes typed during call, not a full transcript]

- been freelancing ~6 years, contract backend work. Higher income than most but VERY lumpy. One big client at a time.
- cancelled Cadence in April. Asked why.
- "It's not that it's bad. I just stopped opening it." Classic. Said he set it up, used it for ~2 months, drifted off.
- Core issue: it never told him anything he didn't already know. "I know I spent money. I want to know if I'm ok."
- Big one: TAXES. He sets aside money for quarterly taxes manually in a separate savings account and it stresses him out. Said if the app automatically carved out a tax % from every payment and showed him "safe to spend" vs "this is the tax man's money" he'd pay double.
  - quote: "the number I actually want is "how much of this is really mine." everything else is noise."
- Mentioned he tried YNAB before, found it too much work / too much manual entry. Same reason he drifted from Cadence — anything that needs daily babysitting loses.
- Did NOT care about: charts, spending categories, social/sharing stuff, the mobile widget.
- Said onboarding was good. Bank sync good. "the bones are fine."
- Would he come back? "if it did the tax thing and the can-I-survive-a-dry-spell thing, yeah, instantly. those are the two things that actually keep me up at night."
- random: complained that support took 4 days to reply once. minor.
