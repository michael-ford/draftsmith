# Q2 in-app survey — raw dump

Sent to active + recently-churned users. 412 responses. Pasting the numbers + a sample of free-text, haven't cleaned this up.

## Who answered
- 71% identify as freelance / self-employed / contractor
- 18% "side income + a job"
- 11% small business owner
- income regularity: 64% said income is "unpredictable month to month"

## Top requested (multi-select, "what would make Cadence more useful?")
- "Tell me how long my money will last" — 58%
- "Automatically set aside money for taxes" — 51%
- "Connect my invoicing / see unpaid invoices" — 44%
- "Better charts / reports" — 12%
- "Shared / household budgeting" — 9%
- "Investment tracking" — 7%

## Churn reasons (people who downgraded/cancelled, n=88)
- "Stopped opening it / forgot about it" — 47%
- "Didn't tell me anything useful" — 23%
- "Too much manual entry" — 19%
- "Too expensive" — 6%
- other — 5%

## Free text (sample, unedited)
- "i want a runway number like a startup has"
- "taxes are the worst part of freelancing and the app ignores them completely"
- "honestly it's just a prettier spreadsheet right now"
- "love the bank sync hate that I have to do everything else myself"
- "stop telling me about coffee"
- "if it knew about my unpaid invoices it would actually be smart"
- "the daily reminders made me anxious so I turned them off and then forgot the app existed"
- "give me one number: am I going to be ok"

## Notes to self
- recurring theme = people want ONE answer, not a dashboard
- engagement is the real problem, retention not acquisition
- the "set aside for taxes" thing keeps coming up unprompted
